Hubra › Modules

Switch on what your club needs.

Hubra is made of modules. Besides members, events and finance there are more, such as bank import, budgets or Discord. Your board switches them on under Settings › Modules; there is nothing to install.

Module

Bank import

Import statements as camt.053, camt.054, MT940 or CSV. Payments are matched to invoices, everything else is booked by your rules.

Module

Budgets

A yearly budget per category, compared with the bookings as you go. Approve it, lock it and copy it to the next year.

Module

Discord

The Hubra bot on your server: announcements, roles, group channels, Discord events, poll buttons and reminders by direct message.

Module

Shared inbox

Read club mailboxes at Infomaniak, Hostpoint or Gmail right in Hubra, each mailbox visible to one group.

Module

Keys

Keys, badges and cards: who holds which, with a handover log. Members see their keys in their account.

Module

Codes

Door codes, lock combinations and passcodes, stored encrypted with your club's key. Shown masked until someone with the right reveals them.

Module

Inventory

Tools, machines and materials: who owns and looks after them, checkouts with due dates, and inductions for items that need one.

Module

Guides

A guide per machine or tool, as text or a link to a manual or video, right on the inventory item.

Module

Board

Tasks per function that pass to the next holder, a year planner of recurring duties, contracts and insurances with notice reminders, and the general meeting from invitation to minutes.

Module

Documents

Your club's document library: folders, uploads and access per role or group, stored encrypted.

Module

Forms

Sign-ups, size lists and surveys: the board builds the form, members fill it in, and the answers come back as a summary, a table or a CSV.

Module

Booking

Rooms and equipment on a calendar: members book a slot, overlaps are refused, the board approves where needed, and a fee goes on the member's invoice.

Module

Kasse

Expense claims with a receipt photo, a drinks and materials tab with QR codes at the fridge, and collective orders for club clothing. Totals go on the member's invoice.

Module

Online payments

Members pay invoices by card or TWINT through your club's own Stripe account. Paid invoices are closed by themselves.

Module

Supporters

Sponsor packages with a yearly invoice and a logo for your website, fundraising goals with a progress bar, donations and member pledges.

Module

Letters

Serial letters for members who read no e-mail: write once, print one PDF for window envelopes, or have Pingen print and post each letter.

Module

Messengers

Announcements in your club's Telegram, Signal or Matrix group: new events, posts and polls, with reminders.

Module

Community

Photo albums that respect who doesn't want to be tagged, a skills and lending directory, an idea box with votes, a welcome checklist, and jubilees.

Module

Teams

Match days for team sports: fixtures from the league's calendar, who can play, the coach's line-up, and carpooling to away games.

Module

Assistant

Writing help for the board: a few notes in, a draft out, in your club's tone and language. Written by Claude with your club's own API key, and only after you agree.

Behind the scenes

New modules without waiting for the next big update.

Many modules are shipped as plugins. That way we improve and update a module without rolling out the whole app again. Nothing changes for you: the module shows up in the list, and you switch it on or off.

  1. We review and install

    Only we install plugins, for the whole platform. Each one is signed and reviewed before it is installed. Clubs never upload code.

  2. You switch it on

    Under Settings › Modules your board switches a module on for your club, like any built-in one. Switched off, it is hidden for everyone and the data stays.

  3. Updates arrive by themselves

    We roll out improvements signed and without interruption for you. If something goes wrong, we go back to the previous version.
