How does an idea get on the roadmap?

  1. Suggest it

    In the club app under Account › "Suggest an idea", or under Settings › Modules at "Missing a module?".

  2. We review it

    The Hubra team reads every idea, merges duplicates and puts it on the roadmap. Who sent it stays private.

  3. Everyone votes

    Anyone with a Hubra account can vote for ideas and planned items. What many need comes sooner.
