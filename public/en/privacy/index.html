Privacy policy

In short

  • We only process data we need to run Hubra. No ads, no analytics, no selling of data.
  • The data is stored on servers in Europe (Contabo, Germany). Some service providers are in the USA; recognised safeguards apply.
  • The club is responsible for the data in a club. We process it on the club's behalf.
  • You can ask for access, correction, deletion and a copy of your data at any time: hi@hubra.club.

1. Who is responsible

The controller for personal data on hubra.club and for Hubra accounts is:

Mike Keller, Hubra (sole proprietorship, not entered in the commercial register)
Grossfeld 17, 6423 Seewen, Switzerland
Email: hi@hubra.club

This policy follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Hubra has not appointed a representative in the EU.

2. Who is responsible for what

Hubra is a platform where many clubs each have their own space. So there are two roles:

  • Hubra is the controller for this website, for your Hubra account (name, email, password, sign-ins, security settings), for messages you send us, for problem reports and ideas, and for contracts and invoices with clubs.
  • The club is the controller for everything in its club space: member data, fees and invoices, events, attendance, votes, groups, inventory, club mail and files. Hubra processes this data only on the club's behalf, under the data processing agreement. Questions about this data are best sent to your club first.

3. Visiting this website

hubra.club is an information site. It sets no tracking cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from third parties. Fonts and images are served by the site itself.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, the time, the page and your browser, to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See GitHub's privacy statement.

If you switch the colour scheme, the site stores your choice in the hubra_theme cookie. Details on the cookies and local storage page.

5. Data in a club

The club decides what it stores in Hubra. Typical data is membership (tier, join date, offices, groups), contact details and address, date of birth (e.g. for J+S), fees, invoices and payments, sign-ups and attendance, votes, checkouts, keys and door codes, posts and comments, and mail in club mailboxes.

We process this data only to run Hubra for the club: storing, backing up, showing it, and sending mails and reminders. We don't analyse it for our own purposes. When a member is deleted permanently, their poll votes stay without a name so results remain correct.

Hubra staff only see club data when support or operations need it. Support access needs a reason, is time-limited, is logged and shows as a banner in the club.

6. Emails and notifications

Hubra sends mails for sign-in (confirmation, password reset, new devices), for the club (invites, invoices, reminders, announcements) and for operations. You choose which club notifications you get in your account. Our mails contain no tracking pixels.

We use a mail service provider (see service providers). It sees sender, recipient, subject and content as far as needed for delivery.

7. Contact, problem reports and ideas

When you write to us or report a problem or suggest an idea in the app, we use what you send to reply and to improve Hubra. Before you send a problem report, the app shows you which technical details (e.g. browser, screen size, app version) go with it. Ideas we show on the public roadmap appear without your name.

8. Clubs as customers

When a club requests or uses Hubra, we process the club's name and address and the names and contact details of its contact people to conclude the contract and invoice. We keep accounting records for ten years, as Swiss law requires (Art. 958f CO). We use Stripe for billing (see service providers). Card details go straight to Stripe; we only see the last four digits and the expiry date.

10. Transfers abroad

Some providers are based in the USA or process data there. We only transfer data to the USA to companies certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, or on the basis of the European Commission's standard contractual clauses as recognised by the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC). Check the providers' certification before publishing.

11. Security

We protect your data with technical and organisational measures, including:

  • encrypted connections (HTTPS) everywhere;
  • separate data spaces per club and a separate key per club for confidential fields;
  • daily backups, encrypted before they leave the server;
  • passwords stored only as hashes, passkeys and two-factor sign-in, required two-factor sign-in for Hubra staff;
  • roles and permissions in the club and a log of changes;
  • support access only with a reason, time-limited and visible to the club.

The full list is in the annex to the data processing agreement. Please report security issues as described in security.txt.

Deleting your account

Write to hi@hubra.club from your account's address and we delete it within 30 days. A self-service delete button in the account is planned. Your club memberships end with it. Anything a club must keep by law (e.g. invoices) stays with the club, without a link to your account.

Leaving a club

You can leave a club in your account. What happens to your data in the club afterwards is up to the club, within its own retention duties.

13. Your rights

You have the right to

  • access your data (Art. 25 FADP, Art. 15 GDPR);
  • have wrong data corrected (Art. 32 FADP, Art. 16 GDPR);
  • have your data deleted (Art. 32 FADP, Art. 17 GDPR) unless we must keep it;
  • restrict processing (Art. 18 GDPR);
  • receive your data in a common format (Art. 28 FADP, Art. 20 GDPR);
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR);
  • withdraw consent at any time, e.g. for push notifications or a linked Discord account.

Write to hi@hubra.club. We reply within 30 days. If your request is about data in a club, we pass it on to the club and help it answer.

You can also complain to a supervisory authority: in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), in the EU the authority where you live.

14. Children and young people

Many clubs have young members. Young people can open their own Hubra account from age 16, younger ones with their parents' consent. The club is responsible for children's data in the club.

15. No automated decisions

Hubra makes no decisions based solely on automated processing that significantly affect you. Only processes the club sets up run automatically, e.g. a payment reminder after the due date.

16. Changes

We update this policy when Hubra or the law changes. The version on this page applies. We tell account holders about significant changes by mail or in the app.
