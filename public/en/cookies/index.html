Cookies and local storage

Hubra only uses cookies needed for sign-in, security and your settings. No tracking, analytics or advertising cookies. That's why there is no cookie banner.

On this website

hubra.club sets no cookie by itself. Only when you switch the colour scheme at the top does the site store your choice:

NamePurposeDuration
hubra_themeLight, dark or automatic. Applies to every Hubra page under hubra.club so the website and the app match.1 year
hubra_theme (local storage)The same choice as a fallback if the browser refuses the cookieuntil you delete it

In the Hubra app

The app at app., sso. and manage.hubra.club needs these cookies. Without them you can't sign in.

NamePurposeDuration
access_token_cookieBeing signed in (short-lived pass)15 minutes
refresh_token_cookieStaying signed in1 day, 30 days with "Keep me signed in"
csrf_access_token, csrf_refresh_tokenProtection against forged requests (CSRF)same as the sign-in
account_token, csrf_account_tokenSign-in on the account page (sso.hubra.club)30 days
hubra_deviceRecognises your browser so we can mail you when you sign in from a new device400 days
remembered_emailFills in your email address at the next sign-in30 days
hubra_themeYour colour scheme1 year
hubra_link_accountSecurity check when linking a Discord account10 minutes

The app also keeps a few settings in your browser's local storage, such as language, colour scheme, sidebar order and favourites, and your club's logo for a quick start. This data doesn't leave your browser.

Legal basis

These cookies are technically necessary to provide the feature you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR, § 25(2) TDDDG, Art. 45c Swiss Telecommunications Act). No consent is required.

Deleting cookies

You can delete cookies and local storage in your browser at any time. You are then signed out and Hubra shows its default settings again.

More on how we process data is in the privacy policy.
