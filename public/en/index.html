In daily use at VollBuild Makerspace. Hubra was built for the everyday work of a real club and runs there every day. Now we're opening it to more clubs.

Features

Everything the board needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Hubra covers the everyday work of a club, from new applications to the annual general meeting. Switch off whatever you don't use.

Members

A directory, profiles and membership applications the board reviews online. Membership tiers with their own fees and payment plans.

Finance

Invoices with the Swiss QR bill, automatic reminders and bookkeeping. Import payments from your e-banking as a camt.054 file.

Events

Training sessions, workshops and parties with sign-up and attendee lists. Every member gets a personal calendar feed.

Polls and votes

Polls and votes for everyone or for selected groups, with proxy votes for members who can't be there.

Groups and offices

Teams, committees and interest groups that members join themselves. Offices, roles and permissions show who looks after what.

Your own club mail

Mailboxes and aliases like president@ or treasurer@ for your club. When an office changes hands, the mailbox goes with it.

Posts

Announcements from the board and posts from the community, visible to everyone or to one group.

Inventory

Tools, machines and materials with checkouts, due dates and inductions. Add keys, badges and deposits if you need them.

Safe by default

One sign-in for everything, encrypted backups, encrypted door codes and an audit log that shows who changed what.

How it works

Your own club space in three steps.

No server and nothing to install. We look after hosting, updates and backups, and you look after your club.

  1. Register your club

    Tell us briefly who you are. We set up your club's space on Hubra.

  2. Set it up

    Pick the modules you need, set up membership tiers and fees, and bring over your current member list.

  3. Invite your members

    Members sign in with a Hubra account. Anyone in several clubs needs only one.

Modules and plugins

Every club builds its own Hubra.

The core features are built in, and each club switches them on or off. Your board installs more features as plugins, just for your club. If you switch something off again, the data stays.

Plugin

Plugin

FAQ

Common questions

Which clubs is Hubra for?

For clubs that have outgrown a spreadsheet: sports clubs, makerspaces, cultural and neighbourhood associations. Hubra was built with VollBuild Makerspace and is in daily use there.

Do we need our own server or an IT person?

No. We run Hubra, including updates and backups. Your board and members only need a browser, on a computer or a phone.

Where is our data stored?

On our servers in Europe, kept apart from other clubs' data. Hubra uses no analytics and shares no data with third parties.

How much does Hubra cost?

The price depends on the size of your club and what you want to use Hubra for. Tell us how many members you have and what you need, and we'll send you an offer. Some plugins may cost extra in the future.

Can we bring our existing members?

Yes. Get in touch with your current list and we'll help you import it.

Can we run Hubra ourselves?

Yes, with Docker Compose or Kubernetes. It mostly makes sense for federations or clubs with their own IT. Write to us and we'll look at it together.

Ready for your clubhouse?

Tell us a little about your club. We'll set up your club's space and help you get started.
