Features

Everything the board needs. Nothing it doesn't.

From the first application to the annual general meeting. Switch off what you don't use, per club, and the data stays.

Members

A directory, profiles and applications the board reviews online. Membership tiers, offices and a detail page for every member.

Fees and invoices

Fee schedules by join date or fixed date, prorated first fees, Swiss QR bills and automatic reminders.

Finance

Bookings with categories, recurring income and expenses, a monthly statement by mail and a yearly budget.

Events and training

One-off events or weekly series, with sign-up, guests, approval and attendance lists. Every member can subscribe to their own calendar.

Polls and votes

Polls and votes for everyone or one group, with proxy votes for members who can't be there.

Groups

Teams, committees and interest groups. Open to join, visible on request, or private.

Roles and permissions

Who can do what? Roles per office or group, down to single machines, events or mailboxes.

Posts and notifications

Announcements and community posts. Reminders arrive by mail, as a push notification or in the app, as each member prefers.

Club mail

Mailboxes and aliases like president@ or treasurer@ for your club. When an office changes hands, the mailbox goes with it.

Inventory and checkouts

Tools, machines and materials with checkouts, due dates, inductions and door codes.

Your look

Club colours, logo, your own mail domain and mails in your club's style. Light or dark, in German or English.

Modules as you need them

Every club switches on what it needs, and every person arranges their own sidebar.

For members

Made for everyone in the club, not just the board.

Members sign up, pay, vote and see their training sessions, on a phone or a computer.

  • One account for several clubsIn the gym club and the makerspace? Sign in once and switch with one tap.
  • Reminders your wayBy mail, push notification or Discord. Every member chooses what they get.
  • Sign in without a passwordWith a passkey, fingerprint or face. Add a second factor for extra protection.
  • Your own calendarAll events and training sessions in your own calendar, on your phone and computer.

Security and privacy

Your members' data is in good hands.

Clubs keep addresses, payments and sometimes data about children. That's why security is built into Hubra, not bolted on.

Hosted in Europe

Servers and backups are in data centres in Europe. We follow the Swiss FADP and the EU GDPR.

A key per club

Every club has its own space and its own keys for confidential data such as door codes and credentials.

Encrypted backups

Daily backups, encrypted before they leave the server. Without the private key nobody can read them, not even the storage provider.

Passkeys and two factors

Passkeys, one-time codes from an app and a mail for every sign-in from a new device. Clubs can require 2FA for their board.

Traceable

An audit log shows the board who changed what and when. When our support steps in, it needs a reason, is time-limited and shows as a banner in the club.

No tracking, no ads

No analytics, no advertising cookies, no selling of data. We earn money by running the service, not from your data.

Plugins

Every club builds its own Hubra.

Your board installs more features as plugins, just for your club. Every plugin is signed and reviewed by us.

Plugin

Bank import

Import statements as camt.053, camt.054, MT940 or CSV. Payments are matched to invoices, everything else is booked by your rules.

Plugin

Budgets

A yearly budget per category, compared with the bookings as you go. Approve it, lock it and copy it to the next year.

Plugin

Discord

The Hubra bot on your server: announcements, roles, group channels, Discord events, poll buttons and reminders by direct message.

Plugin

Shared inbox

Read club mailboxes at Infomaniak, Hostpoint or Gmail right in Hubra, each mailbox visible to one group.

Plugin

Keys

Keys, badges and cards: who holds which, with a handover log. Members see their keys in their account.

Plugin

Guides

A guide per machine or tool, as text or a link to a manual or video, right on the inventory item.

How it works

Your own club space in three steps.

No server and nothing to install. We look after hosting, updates and backups, and you look after your club.

  1. Request your club

    Create a Hubra account and request your club. We review the request and open your space.

  2. Set it up

    Pick your modules, set up tiers and fees and bring over your member list. We help with the import.

  3. Invite your members

    Members join with an invite link. Anyone who already has a Hubra account doesn't need a second one.

FAQ

Common questions

Which clubs is Hubra for?

For clubs that have outgrown a spreadsheet: sports clubs, makerspaces, cultural and neighbourhood associations. Hubra was built with VollBuild Makerspace and is in daily use there.

Do we need our own server or an IT person?

No. We run Hubra, with updates and backups. Your board and members only need a browser, on a computer or a phone.

Where is our data, and who can see it?

On servers in Europe, kept apart from other clubs' data. You decide who in your club sees what. We only process your data on your behalf, as set out in the data processing agreement, and we list every service provider in the privacy policy.

What does Hubra cost?

Hubra is in its pilot phase, and the price depends on the size of your club and what you need. Write to hi@hubra.club and we'll send you an offer.

Can we bring our existing members?

Yes. Get in touch with your current list and we'll help you import it. Your members then get an invite link.

What happens to our data if we leave?

You can export your member list first, and we help with exporting other data. Then we delete your club space. The data disappears from backups after six months at the latest. The details are in the privacy policy.

Can we run Hubra ourselves?

Yes, with Docker Compose or Kubernetes. It mostly makes sense for federations or clubs with their own IT. Write to us and we'll look at it together.

Ready for your clubhouse?

Request your club in two minutes, or tell us first what you need. We'll help you get started.
