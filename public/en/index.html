Features

Everything the board needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Hubra covers the everyday work of a club, from new applications to the annual general meeting. Switch off whatever you don't use.

Members

A directory, profiles and membership applications the board reviews online. Membership tiers with their own fees and payment plans.

Finance

Invoices with the Swiss QR bill, automatic reminders and bookkeeping. Import payments from your e-banking as a camt.054 file.

Events

Training sessions, workshops and parties with sign-up and attendee lists. Every member gets a personal calendar feed.

Polls and votes

Polls and votes for everyone or for selected groups, with proxy votes for members who can't be there.

Groups and offices

Teams, committees and interest groups that members join themselves. Offices, roles and permissions show who looks after what.

Your own club mail

Mailboxes and aliases like president@ or treasurer@ on your own mail server. When an office changes hands, the mailbox goes with it.

Posts

Announcements from the board and posts from the community, visible to everyone or to one group.

Inventory

Tools, machines and materials with checkouts, due dates and inductions. Add keys, badges and deposits if you need them.

Safe by default

One sign-in for everything, encrypted backups, encrypted door codes and an audit log that shows who changed what.

Hosting

Your data, your server.

Hubra isn't a cloud where your member list sits with a vendor. You run it yourself, on a small server in Switzerland or in your own basement.

  • Up in minutesDocker Compose on a single server, or Kubernetes (k3s) with Flux for updates straight from Git.
  • No tracking, no adsNo analytics and no third-party fonts. What happens in the club stays in the club.
  • Made for Swiss clubsQR bills, camt.054 and Swiss German are built in, not bolted on.
# Write a config with fresh secrets
$ scripts/init-env.sh
# Pull the images and start
$ docker compose pull
$ docker compose up -d
# Make yourself the admin
$ docker compose exec backend \
    flask make-admin --email you@club.ch

You get the full guide for your own domains and mail together with access.

Modules and plugins

Switch on only what your club uses.

Every part of Hubra is a module. The board switches modules on and off in the settings, and the data stays. More features arrive as signed plugins, without reinstalling anything.

FAQ

Common questions

Which clubs is Hubra for?

For clubs that have outgrown a spreadsheet: sports clubs, makerspaces, cultural and neighbourhood associations. Hubra was built with a Swiss makerspace and is in daily use there.

Do we need an IT person on the board?

Someone needs to be able to set up a server with Docker. After that, the board only works in the browser. If nobody in your club wants to look after the server, get in touch and we'll find a way together.

Where is our data stored?

On the server you choose. Hubra doesn't send data to us or to anyone else. A built-in backup module keeps encrypted backups.

How much does Hubra cost?

Hubra is currently in a pilot with its first clubs. Write to us if your club would like to join, and we'll talk about the terms directly.

Can we bring our existing members?

Yes. Get in touch with your current list and we'll show you how to import it.

Ready for your clubhouse?

Tell us a little about your club. We'll get back to you with a test account and help you get started.
